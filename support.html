Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do I need to provide my own Gemini API key?

Structura provides the framework for powerful AI tools, but processing is done through your own Google AI account. This ensures you have full control over your usage and data privacy.

Is my data safe?

Yes. The Structura add-on runs entirely within your Google account. Your documents and data are processed by Google's services and are subject to their privacy policies. We do not have access to your files or API keys.
